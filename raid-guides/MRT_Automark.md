## CC caution
Moon (Sheep) and Diamond (Banish) rows will get **re-applied** if the mob is re-targeted, which can **re-break** the CC. If your Mage/Warlock prefers to mark their own CC, delete the Moon/Diamond rows (TK #3–4, SSC #4–5) and keep only the kill/off-tank rows automated.
